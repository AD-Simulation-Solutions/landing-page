Real-world traffic scenarios for ADAS & AD validation

Every road user is a potential ego vehicle.

ADSS turns continuous roadside camera video into classified, simulation-ready traffic scenarios — thousands per hour from a single observation point, delivered in ASAM OpenSCENARIO and OpenDRIVE.

The problem

More synthetic scenarios will not close the evidence gap.

  • {% icon "drift" %}

    Synthetic scenarios drift from reality.

    Generation gives you scale and variation, but frequencies, parameter distributions and relevance can disconnect from what actually happens on real roads.

  • {% icon "occlusion" %}

    Vehicle sensors miss the interaction.

    Ego-mounted perception is excellent for the ego — and blind to everything occluded around it.

  • {% icon "bot" %}

    Simulated traffic is too polite.

    Rule-based NPCs do not reproduce human latency, yielding or unpredictable braking.

The solution

Real traffic, turned into evidence.

One static camera. Six steps. A searchable scenario database instead of a folder of video files.

What you get

  • {% icon "classes" %}

    7 road-user classes

    Cars, vans, trucks, buses, motorcycles, bicycles, pedestrians. Any of them can be the ego.

  • {% icon "gauge" %}

    Criticality built in

    Minimum time-to-collision, distance to critical objects, trajectory curvature.

  • {% icon "cluster" %}

    Corner cases found, not guessed

    Similar events clustered into scenario families; outliers flagged.

  • {% icon "standards" %}

    Open standards

    ASAM OpenSCENARIO, OpenDRIVE and OpenCRG; works with esmini and IPG CarMaker.

Beyond replay

Traffic that reacts.

A recording shows what happened once. Validation asks what happens if the ego brakes harder, arrives earlier or swerves. We are building local traffic behaviour models from observed data, so surrounding traffic responds to the ego instead of replaying a script.
