ODRMaker

OpenDRIVE from aerial imagery, in your browser.

Every camera integration starts with a road model. ODRMaker is a browser-based editor for creating and editing standard ASAM OpenDRIVE.

Features

{% svg "odrmaker" %}
  • {% icon "image" %}

    Imagery as the underlay

    Aerial imagery such as national orthophotos, georeferenced by an origin point, so the road model aligns with real latitude and longitude.

  • {% icon "pen" %}

    Draw, don't code

    Reference lines, lane sections and junction connections, directly in the browser.

  • {% icon "exchange" %}

    Standard in, standard out

    Editable OpenDRIVE; bring your existing HD maps.

  • {% icon "laptop" %}

    Your data stays on your computer

    All the work happens in your browser. Your maps and other files are processed on your computer and never uploaded to a server.
